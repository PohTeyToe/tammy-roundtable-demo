The five-step flow

01 / 05
  1. 01
    Signed agreement Realtor uploads the signed DocuSign agreement (Exclusive Buyer or Seller). The system reads it and starts the flow. DocuSign → Apps Script
  2. 02
    Drive folder A client folder is created with the trade taxonomy you already use. Apps Script → Drive
  3. 03
    Calendar reminders Condition waiver and possession reminders land on the calendar. Apps Script → Calendar
  4. 04
    Trade record + checklist Claude extracts the deal fields and writes the row. The Realtor validation checklist sits at the bottom: signed agreement, FINTRAC, deposit, lawyers, condition waiver, possession, trade record review. Claude → Sheets
  5. 05
    Draft email A client follow-up email is staged in Gmail. Stays a draft until a human sends it. Claude → Gmail (draft only)

What actually runs

  • Realtor uploads the signed DocuSign agreement (Exclusive Buyer or Seller).
  • Apps Script detects buyer or seller side and creates the Drive folder.
  • Claude extracts the trade record fields from the agreement and writes the row.
  • The trade record carries the Realtor validation checklist at the bottom.
  • Calendar reminders cover condition waiver and possession. Gmail draft is staged.
Faked or held back

What stays off for the demo

  • Sample client and property data. No real FINTRAC files.
  • Drafts never auto-send. The script generates and stops.
  • Auth runs against a test Google account, not the production inbox.
  • One missing condition waiver is left visible on purpose.
  • The full live setup is documented in the deploy notes, not on this page.
