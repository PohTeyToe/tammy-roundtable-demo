What This Covers

One operator story, not a stack explanation.

A new transaction moves through document reading, review, folder setup, reminders, and drafted communication in a shape that brokers and realtors can understand quickly. The workflow stays human-reviewed at the points that matter.

Why It Matters
  • Less repeated manual follow-up around a new transaction.
  • Clear document readiness before work moves into the next step.
  • Consistent folder and reminder setup around the deal.
  • Drafted communication without auto-send risk.
Document check

The source is read before work continues.

The operator can see what was found, what is missing, and whether the transaction is ready for review.

Trade record

The row becomes the working record.

Draft email

The client follow-up is staged, not sent.

A grounded draft is prepared from the document and extracted fields, then left for a human to review.
