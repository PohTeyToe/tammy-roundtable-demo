Signed agreement upload

Drop a signed Exclusive Buyer or Seller Agreement PDF. The system reads it, creates the closing folder, seeds the trade record, schedules condition and possession reminders, and drafts the next-step email.

What this is doing

The pipeline runs against your Google account. It will create a "Tammy Roundtable Demo" folder and workbook in your Drive on first run, then for every uploaded agreement: a sub-folder with the closing taxonomy, a row in your trade record sheet, two reminder events on your calendar, and a draft email in your Gmail. Nothing sends.
