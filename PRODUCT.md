# Product Context

## Surface

Static Tammy-facing hosted review surface for the standalone roundtable demo in `C:\VFC\tammy-roundtable-demo`.

## Users

- Tammy as the primary reviewer and presenter
- secondarily, brokers or realtors seeing the workflow during a roundtable or follow-up discussion
- internally, technical reviewers who may use the hosted surface as the clean front door before opening repo docs

## Product Purpose

Show one believable new transaction workflow from intake through drafted communication. The hosted surface should help Tammy explain the operator story quickly, understand where AI helps, and separate the showable workflow from the later live Google proof step without sounding defensive.

## Tone

- practical
- polished
- credible
- calm
- not startup-marketing
- not internal engineering handoff

## Anti-References

- generic SaaS landing page
- purple-gradient AI product aesthetic
- busy dashboard-first framing
- red-warning-heavy “this is not real” proof packet framing
- developer jargon near the top of the page

## Strategic Principles

- Lead with the broker/operator workflow, not the implementation status.
- Make Tammy feel prepared to present, not burdened with caveats.
- Keep the artifact visibly real enough to discuss while remaining honest about the live Google boundary.
- Use typography, spacing, and hierarchy to make the page feel intentional and high-trust.
