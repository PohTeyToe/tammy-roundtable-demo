# Design Context

## Design Direction

Editorial briefing for a high-trust brokerage workflow demo.

This surface should feel closer to a polished deal memo or presentation board than to a SaaS marketing page. The right mood is warm, precise, and quietly premium.

## Visual Rules

- Use a serif-led typographic system with a technical mono accent.
- Prefer parchment, stone, ink, and muted green over flat white or loud saturated palettes.
- Avoid dense card walls when a stronger narrative section can do the job.
- Use asymmetry and sectional rhythm so the page feels designed, not templated.
- Keep color use selective and meaningful.

## Content Rules

- Put the workflow story first.
- Translate implementation concepts into operator language whenever possible.
- Keep one short honest note about demo-data or live-auth boundaries, but do not let that note dominate the page.
- Treat the packet as the deeper layer, not the homepage thesis.

## Motion

- Keep motion light and purposeful.
- Prefer subtle load-in fades and small hover transitions.
- Do not use flashy looping animation.
